# Interview Training Tips

## PAR Method (Problem → Action → Result)

Use the **PAR method** instead of STAR for behavioral interview questions. It covers the same information but is easier to remember with three logical steps.

### The Three Steps

1. **Problem** - Identify what you noticed
2. **Action** - Describe what you did
3. **Result** - Share the outcome

### Maximize Impact: Be Proactive at Every Step

#### Problem: Proactive Discovery
Show that you actively identify issues rather than waiting for them to be assigned.

**Example:**
"I noticed that we were storing user consent in two locations, leading to inconsistent states and legal exposure."

#### Action: Proactive Initiative
Demonstrate that you take ownership and drive solutions forward.

**Example:**
"I reached out to my manager, explained what I found, and suggested that we prioritize creating a single source of truth."

#### Result: Show Business Impact
Emphasize ownership and quantify the benefit to the organization.

**Example:**
"Management agreed, and I led a team that ultimately resolved the data inconsistency issues. The company now has a clearer, more accurate consent tracking system that reduces legal risk."

### Key Principles

- **Proactivity wins** - At every step, show you took initiative
- **Own the narrative** - Use "I" to show your direct contribution
- **Business value** - Always tie the result back to organizational benefit
- **Be specific** - Concrete examples are more memorable than general statements
