Phase 1: Requirements Gathering

"Walk us through your approach..." → ASK QUESTIONS FIRST

Category Questions to Ask
ScaleVolume? Docs/day? Concurrent users?
LatencyReal-time or batch? Acceptable wait time?
DataDocument types? Quality? Formats?
ComplianceHIPAA? PII? Regulatory requirements?
ConstraintsExisting stack? Budget? Team size?
Pain PointsWhat's broken? Why redesign now?
Transition: "Let me summarize: [constraints]. Let me sketch the architecture."

Phase 2: High-Level Design

You drive. Silence = they're waiting for you to continue.

// Walk through data flow in order:
1. ENTRY → How does data get in?
2. QUEUE → How decouple ingestion/processing?
3. PROCESS → What does the work?
4. STORAGE → Where does data live?
5. OUTPUT → Where do results go?
6. FAILURE → DLQ, retries, alerts?
Signal you're done: They start asking "Why X over Y?" or "Let's dive into..."

Phase 3: Deep Dive

They pick 1-2 areas and drill. Back-and-forth.

Common Topics
  • • Scaling (10x load)
  • • Failure handling
  • • Data consistency
  • • Migration strategy
  • • Compliance/PII flow
  • • "Why X over Y?"
Answer Framework
  1. 1. State your choice
  2. 2. Explain WHY
  3. 3. Acknowledge trade-off
  4. 4. Justify the trade-off
Example: "I'd use SQS over Redis because we can't lose claims. Trade-off is latency, but we're batch processing so that's fine."

🚨 Emergency Moves

Stuck "Let me think through this out loud..."
Don't know "I haven't worked with that directly—here's my mental model, but I'd validate with the team"
Mistake "Actually, let me correct myself..." (then fix it)
Buy time "Good question. Before I answer—[ask clarifying Q]"

⭐ The One Rule

You drive Phases 1-2. Silence = keep going.

They drive Phases 3-4. Silence = they're thinking.
