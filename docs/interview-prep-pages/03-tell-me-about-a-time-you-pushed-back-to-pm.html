3. Tell me about a time you pushed back (STAR, 2 min)

  1. At The Penny Hoarder, our DevOps engineer wanted to migrate our WordPress site from pure Docker to Kubernetes with full orchestration.
  2. I pushed back respectfully by asking what problem this solved - we were a content site with a serverless database on RDS, not a high-transaction app needing Kubernetes complexity.
  3. I explained my concerns about adding unnecessary complexity when we didn't need orchestration at our scale, but suggested my manager give him space for a proof of concept.
  4. In the end, the implementation and deployment complexity became a blocker, and we stayed with Docker.

STAR breakdown:

  • Situation: DevOps engineer wanted to migrate WordPress from Docker to Kubernetes
  • Task: Evaluate if complexity justified for content site needs
  • Action: Asked what problem it solved, explained unnecessary complexity, suggested POC
  • Result: Implementation complexity became blocker, stayed with Docker
