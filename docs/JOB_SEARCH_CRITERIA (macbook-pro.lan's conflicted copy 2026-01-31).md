## 🏢 Company Characteristics

### Company Size & Stage

- **Size**: Flexible (10-1000+ employees) if other criteria are met
- **Stage**: Prefer established companies with proven business models
- **Funding**: Stable funding or profitable (avoid pre-revenue startups)

### Red Flags (Explicit Evidence Required)

**CRITICAL: Red flags must be based on explicit evidence, not speculation or unknowns.**

Only flag as red when there is clear evidence of:

- **Documented layoffs** (with dates and scale)
- **Poor management ratings** (below 3.0/5 with recent reviews)
- **High employee turnover** (documented in reviews or news)
- **Financial instability** (bankruptcy filings, funding issues, revenue problems)
- **Toxic culture** (multiple recent reviews citing specific problems)
- **Required on-call** (explicitly stated in job posting)
- **People management required** (explicitly stated as "manage direct reports" or similar)
- **Required office days** (explicitly contradicts fully remote)

### Job Posting Analysis Guidelines

**Treat unknowns as neutral facts, not negatives:**

- Missing salary range → "Salary: Not listed"
- No mention of on-call → "On-call: Not mentioned"
- Remote not explicitly stated → "Remote status: Not explicitly confirmed"
- Tech stack variety → List it factually without judgment

**Present concerns factually:**
- State what IS known from the job posting
- Separate facts from interpretation
- Don't speculate about company problems without evidence
- Ask clarifying questions rather than assuming negatives

### Positive Indicators

- Strong engineering culture
- Focus on code quality and best practices
- Mentorship opportunities
- Clear career progression paths
- Employee development investment
- Stable business model with growth potential
- Good work-life balance ratings (4.0/5+)

## 🔍 Research Priorities

When evaluating companies, always check:

- Recent employee reviews (last 6-12 months)
- Layoff history and financial stability
- Management quality ratings
- Work-life balance scores
- Salary transparency on Glassdoor/Levels.fyi
- Company news and funding status
