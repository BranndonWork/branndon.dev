⚡ Energy Management

Before: "I need 1-2 offers. This is 1 data point."

During: Over-explaining? → STOP, breathe, facts only. Lean back physically.

After: Wait 24hrs, brief thank-you, move on mentally.

🎬 Interview Flow (Every Time)

  1. Small Talk - Match their energy, don't exceed
  2. Listen to Pitch - What problem does hiring solve?
  3. My Background - STAR stories anchored to their problem
  4. Technical - Honest, show problem-solving approach
  5. My Questions - Ask 2-3 prepared questions
  6. Close - "This sounds like a good fit" ✓

❌ NOT: "I really want this!"

❓ Standard Questions to Ask

  1. "What does success look like in first 6 months?"
  2. "What's the biggest technical challenge facing the team?"
  3. "Why is this role open - new headcount or backfill?"

🎤 Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

"Senior SWE with 15 years building APIs and backend systems. Strong in Python/Django. I've done architecture/mgmt but happiest as IC shipping quality code. Looking to dive deep technically."

🛡️ Common Objections (Scripts)

"Overqualified?"

→ "I'm happiest as an IC focused on coding. I've done architecture/management and intentionally seeking senior engineering."

"Why looking?"

→ "I'm being strategic about my next role to ensure it's the right fit technically and culturally."

"Why not lead/principal?"

→ "I'm most effective hands-on coding. I prefer being deep in technical work."

"Salary expectations?"

→ "I don't have a specific number. Focused on finding the right fit - confident we can work out fair compensation."

"Tell me about failure"

→ Use prepared story: failure → learning → better outcome. Stay professional.

🔒 Information Management

✓ Be Honest:

  • Skills I lack (willing to learn)
  • Why I prefer IC over lead
  • What I'm looking for

✗ Don't Volunteer:

  • How long looking
  • Financial pressure
  • Past salary
