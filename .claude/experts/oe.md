# OE Expert — branndon.dev Job Search Project

## Context
Branndon is actively pursuing over-employment (OE) — holding multiple full-time remote positions concurrently. OE viability is the primary ranking factor for all job evaluations, above salary.

## Scoring System
Full matrix in `docs/over-employment-oe-knowledgebase.md`. Summary:

| Category | Max | What It Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Work Measurement Model | 25 | Output vs presence monitoring |
| Meeting Load Delta from SSE Baseline | 25 | Above/below ~10 hrs/week |
| Schedule & On-Call Constraints | 25 | Flexibility, on-call, timezone locks |
| Organizational Camouflage | 25 | Company size, visibility, load-bearing risk |

Baseline SSE (~80-90% of roles): code reviews, standard Agile ceremonies, git/CI/CD, 40hr nominal employment. Do NOT penalize for these — they are universal.

## Hard Disqualifiers (Apply Before Scoring)
| Disqualifier | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Primary on-call responsibility | -20 pts |
| Surveillance/activity monitoring confirmed | -20 pts |
| Billable hours / consulting / staffing model | -20 pts |
| Security clearance investigation required | -15 pts |
| In-office requirements >1 day/week | -15 pts |
| Series A or earlier startup | -10 pts |

## Bonuses
- Globally distributed team (natural async): +5
- Mature/maintenance-mode product: +5
- PE-owned or large bureaucratic parent org: +5
- No equity offered (low "ownership" pressure): +3

## OE-Hostile JD Phrases
"Fast-paced environment", "wear multiple hats", "hands-on", "work hard play hard", "collaborative culture", "flat organization", "startup mentality", "tight-knit team", "urgency and ownership"

## Current OE Target Profile
- Ideal: 1,000+ employee company, distributed team, mature SaaS product, async-first, results-based evaluation
- Acceptable: 200–1,000 employees with strong async signals
- Risky: <200 employees, Series B or earlier, or any on-call rotation
